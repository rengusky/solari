Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

This policy covers solari.rangarajan.design (“the Site”), the landing page for the Solari flip-clock app. In short: the Site is a static page that sets no cookies, has no accounts or forms, runs no analytics or advertising, and does not collect personal information about you. It loads no third-party fonts, scripts, or trackers — the typeface is served from this site itself. The clock and its sound run entirely in your browser, using your device’s own local time.

What is processed when you visit

A couple of third parties are unavoidably involved when the page is delivered or when you buy the app:

  • Hosting & security — Cloudflare. The Site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare automatically processes technical request data — including your IP address, browser type, and timestamps — to deliver the page and protect against abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Purchases — Lemon Squeezy. If you choose to download or buy the app, checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Any payment and contact details you enter there are processed by Lemon Squeezy under their privacy policy — not by this Site.

Cookies & local storage

None. The Site stores nothing on your device — no cookies, no local storage, no tracking identifiers.

Your choices & rights

Because the Site itself collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. For data processed by Cloudflare, Google, or Lemon Squeezy, please refer to their respective privacy policies and contacts linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; when we do, the “last updated” date above will change.
